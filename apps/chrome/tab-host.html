Tab audio interpreter

All audio from the tab where you invoked Interpreter is captured, including frames, advertisements and simultaneous sounds. Other tabs and microphones are excluded. No video selection is required.

Prepare the language here, then Start. Keep this window open and visible. After Stop, capture the original tab again before preparing. Navigation or tab closure requires a fresh extension action.
