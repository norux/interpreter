Selected video interpreter

Confirm a video from the page where you opened Interpreter. Prepare the language here, then Start and allow audio on the video page. Keep this window open and visible.

Only ordinary videos in the page's top frame are supported by this build. Other frames and protected or unverified media need their own supported adapter.

Captions follow the selected video inline and in a fullscreen player container. Video-only fullscreen hides page captions; the comparison window remains available.
